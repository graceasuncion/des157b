Comparative Analysis

Coming Out Simulator

Nicky Case  ·  ncase.me/cos

Coming Out Simulator is a short interactive narrative by Nicky Case based on a true story about coming out to his family during a tense phone call. The interface is intentionally bare, with plain text, a dark background, and simple choice buttons. This puts more emotional weight on the words rather than the visuals. The dialogue presents no correct answer, which makes the user's choice feel like a real one. It stays with you because of how it’s specific to one person’s story.

Syrian Journey: Choose Your Own Escape Route

BBC News  ·  bbc.co.uk/news/world-middle-east-32057601

Syrian Journey is an interactive web experience produced by BBC News in 2015, based on real interviews and documented stories from Syrian refugees making the journey from Syria to Europe. The interface uses a clean and editorial layout, with short blocks of text followed by two to three choices. It feels like journalism more than a game, keeping the tone serious. The choices are genuinely hard with no safe option, making the player feel personally responsible for the outcome.
