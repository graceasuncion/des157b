Difficulties & Challenges

Drawing the context

I'm planning on drawing the grandma, who will be speaking to you, and the background imagery. I feel like this would take a while, as usually the context itself takes me the longest to do.

Balancing emotion with usability

For this project, I do want to enable this emotion of not being able to understand a language, without wanting to make the user disengage from my project. It's more of figuring out how to pace everything, which I feel that user testing will help me with.

Scope risk

With this project, there is an opportunity to expand it as I build it. Given the time constraints, it's not feasible to add any more ideas. There must be priority with the features that I already have planned out, and I shouldn't get ahead of myself.
