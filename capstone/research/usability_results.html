Usability Results

Observations

1. Lack of onboarding content

When going to the landing page, users didn't have enough context to understand what kind of experience they were getting themselves into. The title and the button didn't communicate what was going to happen next and made users confused.

2. Response options were unclear

Users didn't know what to do with the response buttons at the bottom of the screen during the exchanges. They didn't know what the options did and how they interacted with the rest of the narrative.

3. Glitch between the ending dial and pre-prompts

There was a noticeable visual glitch in the transition from the ending dial and the pre-prompts, despite this being something that I thought I fixed before user testing.

Analysis

Users were able to navigate the project, but not without initial confusion. The experience does not provide enough direction at the start, which created hesitation before users were able to figure it out themselves. The three main areas that need improvement are the onboarding experience, the response option clarity, and the transition between the ending dial and the pre-prompt. The usability feedback was primarily focused on interaction issues rather than the emotional content, which suggests that the friction was significant enough to occupy users' attention before the intended message could come through. Making these adjustments will hope to clarify the emotional impact for the narrative.

Plan of Action

1. Add onboarding content to the landing screen

Add a brief description and instruction so users understand what they are entering and what will be expected of them during the interaction.

2. Improve response button clarity

Improve the visual design of the response buttons to make their interactivity more apparent and signal to the user that a choice is required.

3. Resolve the transition glitch

Continue debugging the transition between the ending dial screen and the pre-prompts to fully resolve the glitch.
